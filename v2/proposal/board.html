V2 · Visual direction proposal · 1 October 2026

Clear Light.

A calm, architectural direction for MNNJ: daylight photography, one typeface, one accent, one signature interaction. The site should look like the result of the work.

For approval before any development.
Photography shown is placeholder direction, not MNNJ work.

01 · Diagnosis

Why the current V2 doesn't work.

  1. The first impression is dirt.Fog, droplets and grime over the hero make the brand look murky, the opposite of what MNNJ sells.
  2. Nothing looks photographed.One AI dusk image reused everywhere, plus procedural paving and grass that read as CGI.
  3. Too many typographic voices.Four families, heavy weights and mono micro-labels on every edge. No hierarchy, only noise.
  4. Interface clutter.Percent meters, rails, chips, hints and a tool cursor make it read as a game, not a premium service.
  5. No colour system.Muddy fog greys, dusk orange and electric blue compete. Blue is used as a fill instead of an accent.
  6. Technique replaced design.Shaders and particles were asked to carry the brand. Gag copy ("This screen needs a clean") undercut it.

03 · Direction

Clear Light: five principles.

01

Daylight, clarity, calm

The site itself should look freshly cleaned: bright silver-white space, crisp edges, nothing murky.

02

Photography first

Architecture and materials (stone, glass, roofline, lawn) shown beautifully, never covered with effects.

03

One typeface, set with care

Instrument Sans at regular weight; hierarchy from scale and space.

04

One accent: the clean line

Electric blue appears as a thin line, like a squeegee's edge or the border between before and after.

05

One signature interaction

The "clean line" reveal in the hero, plus quiet image reveals. No particles, meters or game UI.

04 · Typography

Instrument Sans, 400 and 500.

A contemporary neo-grotesk with a slight width axis: architectural, calm and legible. Free Google Font, self-hostable. One family across the site.

07 · Signature interaction

The clean line.

The cleaning idea survives as one precise gesture rather than a mess of effects. A single electric-blue line, like the edge of a squeegee, follows the pointer across the hero and leaves the image clean behind it.

HeroDrag or hover moves the line; touch drags sideways; arrow keys step it. Labelled "Illustrative demo".
Services indexHovering a row slides the name 24px and reveals one photograph beside the cursor.
ChaptersImages settle from 103% to 100% scale; text appears with opacity and a 12px rise over 700ms.
Page transitionsA single vertical line wipes between pages, the same "clean line".
Reduced motionEverything static; the hero line sits at 50% with a button to compare.
NeverParticles, droplets, fog, progress meters, custom tool cursors, game prompts.

08 · Real photography

What a one-day shoot would unlock.

Commissioned photos of real MNNJ work, with the homeowner's consent, would replace every placeholder and make the before/after genuine.

  1. 01Hero elevation

    A serviced home in daylight, three-quarter view, straight verticals, sky and lawn in frame.

  2. 02Paving before/after

    Same patio, locked-off tripod, identical framing and light: the only honest way to show the clean line.

  3. 03Glass & conservatory

    Exterior glazing with clear reflections, raking light, frames crisp.

  4. 04Roofline & gutters

    Gutter run shot from below against open sky, after clearing.

  5. 05Garden

    Low-angle lawn stripes and clipped hedges, morning light.

  6. 06Craft details

    Tools, hands and equipment, no faces, if MNNJ wants a human note. Never a stand-in for Marvin.
